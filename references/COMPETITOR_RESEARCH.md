# Design references and role assignment

## Primary style reference (principles only)
- **Virtuo**: https://www.govirtuo.com/ | Booking-first clarity, reduced navigation, restrained color, compact browsing experience. DO NOT scrape and republish its content, source code, photos, or branding.

## Secondary UX references
- **Centauro**: https://www.centauro.net/en/ | Practical rental search, clear vehicle classes, booking form patterns.
- **SIXT**: https://www.sixt.com/ | Strong multi-location date selection and filtration, but do not copy its dense merchandising.

## Original Kalimeris sources (unverified client identity)
- Existing similarly named business: https://kalimeriscars.gr/ | Public corporate site, not a license to copy.
- Rental postings: https://kalimeris.car.gr/classifieds/ | Candidate fleet research only. The rental listing prices may be indicative. No unauthorized bulk copying.

## Inspection workflow
Use a browser to inspect public pages, record route structure, viewport screenshots, navigation states and general visual patterns. Respect access restrictions and rate limits. Extract principles, not code/assets. Record observation/source/rationale for each adopted feature.

## Main differences from Virtuo
No app-only promise, no proprietary remote unlock claims, no fake self-service automation. Kalimeris should feel similarly frictionless but use a genuine rental inquiry flow until a booking provider is integrated.
